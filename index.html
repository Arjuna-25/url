About Dr. Kiran Nagaraj

Dr. Kiran Nagaraj is a highly experienced Orthopedic and Joint Replacement Surgeon with over 20 years of dedicated service in the field of orthopedics. He specializes in complex joint replacement surgeries, sports injuries, and trauma management.

With a patient-centric approach and cutting-edge techniques, Dr. Nagaraj has successfully treated thousands of patients, helping them regain mobility and improve their quality of life.
